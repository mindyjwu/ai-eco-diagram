About the AI Ecosystem

Who actually pays for AI?

You do, a few dollars at a time. Shopping, feeds, meals, trips, classes, paychecks, doctors and chatbots all run on AI. This map follows that money down through labs, clouds, chips, factories, power and rockets to a quartz mine in North Carolina.

Caveats

Educational, not investment advice. Facts are to about mid-2026 and some will have moved. Outlooks are qualitative; use each panel’s quote link for current figures.

Links are typical, publicly known relationships, simplified, not a list of contracts. Logos come from each company’s own site; trademarks belong to their owners.
