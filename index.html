Four ways into this map

Own a piece

Public companies show up with a ticker and a live-quote link. Think about which layer you understand best and whether you are paying for a chokepoint or just a story.

Work inside it

Fabs, power, cooling and grid construction are short of skilled people. Chip design, ML and infrastructure roles sit across every layer.

Build on it

Open each layer’s “What to know” box for a builder’s angle. Private companies are your potential partners, customers or competitors.

Finance or govern it

Datacentres run on private credit and sovereign money, and the bottom of the chain is shaped by export controls and mining policy.

Every layer on this map was once someone’s bold bet. A food company ended up holding the key film inside AI chips. A rocket company rewrote launch costs. The next one could be yours: be ambitious, be curious, and look for the layer others are ignoring.

Read this first. This is an educational map, not investment advice. Descriptions reflect my knowledge to roughly mid-2026 and some details will have moved; check the live-quote and website links before acting on anything. “How they’re doing” is qualitative on purpose.
